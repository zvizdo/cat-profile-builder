05

Motion & conduct

The profile moves on scroll. The builder never does — it responds.
Timing
180ms · hover, focus, chip toggle ease-out 320ms · panel open, block select, reorder settle 1000ms · profile section rise cubic-bezier(.16,.84,.28,1) 6.5s · one cat on the carousel
Reduced motion

Same layout, same order, same copy — scroll-driven scenes resolve to their end state, the carousel stops auto-advancing and pages by arrow key, and nothing autoplays. The design is never carried by the animation.

Five rules
One accent per screen. If two things are blue, one of them is wrong. The photograph is the design. Type gets out of its way. Every irreversible action names what it will remove first. No spinners on the public profile — striped placeholders and plain sentences instead. A notice says what happened, in one sentence, with the way out in it.

These twelve are geometric on purpose: dots, bars, chevrons, one triangle, one arc. Anything needing a real metaphor — a cat, a vet record, a share sheet — comes from Lucide at 20px / 1.5px stroke, unfilled, and is never recoloured except to #0B6FB4 for an active state or #A4552A for a destructive one. No icon appears without a label in the tools; the wall uses no icons at all.

07

Notices & media rules

Toasts report. Modals ask. Inline notes explain. Nothing shouts.
Toasts · bottom-left, 6s, one at a time
Gallery photo added. Undo
Uploading rain-day.mov — 2 of 3 68%
That photo is 640px wide — too small for the hero. Use anyway
Upload failed. Nothing was added. Try again
Success and progress dismiss themselves. Warnings and errors stay until dismissed and never block the canvas. A toast that offers Undo keeps its offer for the full 6s.
Modals · only when the answer changes what happens
Remove the gallery? Three photos come off Charlotte's page. They stay in your media library, and one undo brings the section back.
Keep it Remove section
That clip is 2:07. The wall plays 12 seconds. Pick the twelve seconds worth watching. We've guessed the steadiest stretch — drag to change it.
0:28 – 0:40 · muted · loops
Cancel Use this stretch
Two buttons, right-aligned, the safe one on the left as plain outline. The destructive one is clay and names the object. Escape always equals the left button. Never a modal to say something worked.
Media rules
photosJPEG, PNG, HEIC. Up to 25MB each, 1600px wide minimum for a hero, 1200px for gallery. 12 per gallery.
videoMP4, MOV, up to 500MB and 3 minutes on upload. Trimmed to 4–12s for the wall, 30s for the profile. Always muted, always looping.
cropEvery photo carries a focal point set on upload. Crops are derived from it, never centre-cropped blind.
alt textRequired before publish. One sentence, written by the volunteer, offered as a draft they can accept or rewrite.
failureAn upload never partially applies. If it fails, nothing changed and the toast says so.
too smallA warning, not a block. Volunteers photograph cats on old phones and the alternative is no photo.
