What changes on touch The rail becomes a horizontal strip of 44px pills. Block actions stop being 10px mono links and become pills inside the selected block — a finger can't hit "duplicate" at 10px. Theme and Media open as sheets. Drag stays, but every block also has explicit Move up / Move down, because dragging a full-width block on a tablet while the page scrolls is unreliable.
Breakpoints
≥1440 — full: 250px rail, canvas, 360px helper 1180–1439 — helper docks to a 52px tab, opens as overlay 1024–1179 — touch layout: top strip, pill actions, helper as bubble <1024 — read-only preview + "Open on a laptop or tablet to edit"
Why not a phone builder Volunteers photograph cats on phones, so the phone job is upload and review — both of which the read-only view plus an upload button cover. Editing a 1440-wide page layout on a 390 screen would need a different product, not a smaller one.

Offline The wall keeps looping on yesterday's cats. The last good rotation is cached, so a dropped connection never blanks the screen at an event. A small mono line dates what's showing.
Reduced motion · kiosk No drift, no auto-advance, arrow keys page it. Same composition, held still. Space pauses, and a volunteer can walk a family through the cats by hand.
