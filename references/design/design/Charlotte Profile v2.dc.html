Who she is

An explorer who never gives up, and will loudly ask for pets.

Charlotte came in from a laundromat parking lot in April, thin and unbothered. Two weeks later she had claimed the highest shelf in the foster room and started supervising the household from it.

She is a talker. Not a complainer — a negotiator. She will follow you room to room stating her case for attention, and once you sit down she settles against your leg and goes quiet, as if the matter had been resolved reasonably.

What she needs in a home

Three things, and she'll take care of the rest herself.

01

A window she can own

Any sill will do, as long as it's hers and it faces something that moves.

02

Someone who talks back

She is a conversationalist. A quiet house suits her; a silent one doesn't.

03

Patience for week one

She hides for a few days, then decides you're staff. Cats and confident kids are fine.
